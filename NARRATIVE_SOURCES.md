## 2. Star Atlas Germany material

Basis: the supplied SAG Whitepaper. Only public-safe principles are used:

- SAG is an independent, community-driven Web3 gaming DAC.
- SAG has no direct organizational connection to Star Atlas or ATMTA.
- SAG is multifactional across MUD, ONI and Ustur.
- SAG is peaceful, partnership-oriented and open to the wider German-speaking community.
- Freedom, personal responsibility, integrity, respect, privacy, solidarity, mentoring and sustainability are core values.
- Professional paths include resources, manufacturing, logistics, trade, defense, exploration, data work, repair and rescue.

Confidential treasury, token allocation and detailed internal governance mechanisms are deliberately excluded from the public game.

These elements are labelled `SAG DAC` in the game.

## 3. Original SAG Frontier fiction

The following are original game elements and are not official Star Atlas lore:

- The Echo of Iris signal
- Wardens and the Warden Protocol
- The Black Signal
- The SAG Command Network as depicted in this game
- Mara Voss, Eno Vael and U-19
- All campaign-specific incidents and dialogue branches

These elements are labelled `SAG FRONTIER FIKTION` in the game.

## Narrative rule

Official canon may provide the setting and historical pressure, but it must not be rewritten to imply that SAG is an official Star Atlas organization. SAG-specific claims and original fiction must remain visibly labelled.
